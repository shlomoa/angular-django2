Tabs

Output of ng generate angular-django2:tabs for the two Tabs nodes of tabs-demo.openui.json.

Horizontal tab group

uses.orientation horizontal compiles to a Material tab group. The Archive tab is disabled (uses.disabled).

Vertical tab list

uses.orientation vertical with uses.selectedIndex 1 compiles to an ARIA tablist; Arrow Up, Arrow Down, Home and End move the selection.
